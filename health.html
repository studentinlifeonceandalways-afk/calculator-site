BMI Calculator

Use this BMI calculator to estimate your Body Mass Index from your height and weight in metric units. BMI is a screening tool that compares your weight to a range considered healthy for your height in adults 20 and older. It is not a diagnostic tool and does not measure body fat directly, muscle mass, or distribution of fat.

Worked example

70 kg at 175 cm:

  • BMI: 22.9
  • Category: Normal weight

A BMI of 22.9 falls in the healthy range (18.5–24.9) for adults.

BMI categories for adults

  • Underweight: below 18.5
  • Normal weight: 18.5 – 24.9
  • Overweight: 25.0 – 29.9
  • Obese: 30.0 and above

Assumptions and limitations

  • Intended for adults 20 and older; not valid for children or teens
  • Does not distinguish muscle from fat; athletes may show high BMI with low body fat
  • Does not account for age, sex, ethnicity, or fat distribution
  • Not a substitute for medical advice or clinical assessment

How the calculation works

Formula: BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)2

Height in centimeters is divided by 100 to convert to meters before squaring. This is the standard World Health Organization formula for adult BMI.

Frequently asked questions

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

No. BMI is a population-level screening tool. It can misclassify muscular athletes as overweight and may not reflect health risk accurately across different ethnic groups. It should be used alongside other measures such as waist circumference and clinical assessment.

Should I use BMI for children?

No. Children and teens require age- and sex-specific BMI percentile charts, not the adult categories used here. Consult a pediatrician for child growth assessment.

What is a healthy BMI?

For most adults, a BMI between 18.5 and 24.9 is considered normal weight. However, "healthy" depends on individual factors including muscle mass, age, and medical history. Discuss your results with a healthcare professional.
