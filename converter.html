Unit Converter

Use this unit converter to convert between common length, weight, and temperature units. Select a category, enter a value, and choose the "from" and "to" units. Conversions use standard international definitions and are accurate to six decimal places.

Worked example

1 mile to kilometers:

  • Result: 1.609344 km

1 mile is defined as exactly 1,609.344 meters.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Length conversions use international definitions (e.g., 1 inch = 25.4 mm)
  • Weight conversions use avoirdupois weight (e.g., 1 lb = 0.45359237 kg)
  • Temperature conversions use exact Celsius/Fahrenheit/Kelvin relationships
  • Rounding to six decimal places; very large or very small values may show rounding artifacts

How the calculation works

Length and weight: value × factor(from) ÷ factor(to), where factors are in base units (meters, kilograms).

Temperature: convert to Celsius first, then to the target unit using the standard formulas.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between mass and weight?

Strictly, mass is the amount of matter (kg), while weight is the force of gravity on it (newtons). In everyday use, "weight" in pounds or kilograms is treated as mass, which is what this converter uses.

Is a US gallon the same as a UK gallon?

No. A US gallon is 3.785 liters; a UK (imperial) gallon is 4.546 liters. This converter does not currently include volume units.

How accurate are the conversions?

Length and weight conversions use exact defined factors and are accurate to the precision of floating-point math. Temperature conversions are exact within the same limits.
