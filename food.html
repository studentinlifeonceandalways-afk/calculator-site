Recipe Scaler

Use this recipe scaler to adjust an ingredient quantity when you change the number of servings a recipe makes. Enter the original quantity, the original serving count, and the new serving count. The calculator returns the scaled quantity and the scale factor. Works for any unit — grams, cups, tablespoons — because it only scales the number.

Worked example

2 cups for 4 servings, scaled to 6 servings:

  • Scale factor: 1.5×
  • New quantity: 3 cups

Assumptions and limitations

  • Assumes the recipe scales linearly — true for most ingredients
  • Does not adjust for evaporation, pan size, or leavening agents, which often need separate adjustment
  • Does not handle unit conversion — use the Unit Converter for that

How the calculation works

Scale factor = New servings ÷ Original servings

New quantity = Original quantity × Scale factor

Frequently asked questions

Do all ingredients scale the same way?

No. Salt, spices, leavening agents (baking powder, yeast), and alcohol often need less than a linear scale-up. For baking especially, adjust these manually and check texture as you go.

Can I scale a recipe down?

Yes. Enter a new serving count smaller than the original and the scale factor will be below 1. Very small batches may need extra care with leavening and pan size.

Does this convert cups to grams?

No. This scales the number only. Use the Unit Converter for unit-to-unit conversion, and note that cup-to-gram conversion depends on the ingredient's density.
