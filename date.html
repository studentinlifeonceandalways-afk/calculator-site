Age Calculator

Use this age calculator to find exact age in years, months, and days from a date of birth to today. It also shows total months and total days. Useful for forms, anniversaries, and eligibility checks where exact age matters.

Worked example

Born May 10, 1990; today November 23, 2026:

  • Exact age: 36 years, 6 months, 13 days
  • Total months: 438
  • Total days: 13,346

Assumptions and limitations

  • Uses the local time zone of your device
  • Treats dates at midnight; leap years are handled automatically
  • Months are counted as calendar months, not fixed 30-day periods

How the calculation works

The calculator subtracts the birth date from today's date in calendar units: years first, then months, then days, borrowing from the previous month when the day difference is negative.

Total days is the exact difference between the two dates in days.

Frequently asked questions

Does this account for leap years?

Yes. Leap years are handled automatically because the calculation uses real calendar dates.

Why does the "months" count sometimes differ from a simple division?

Calendar months vary in length. This calculator counts full calendar months between the two dates, which is how age is typically described on official forms.

Can I calculate age on a future date?

Not with this version — it calculates age as of today. Future-date age is typically used for eligibility or retirement planning and requires a different calculator.
