Percentage Calculator

Use this percentage calculator to find what X percent of Y is. Enter the percentage and the number, and the calculator returns the result. For percent change or percent increase/decrease, see the formula and FAQ sections below.

Worked example

15% of 200:

  • Result: 30

15 ÷ 100 = 0.15; 0.15 × 200 = 30.

How the calculation works

X% of Y = (X ÷ 100) × Y

Percent change = ((New − Old) ÷ Old) × 100

Percent increase = ((New − Old) ÷ Old) × 100 (positive result)

Percent decrease = ((Old − New) ÷ Old) × 100 (positive result)

Assumptions and limitations

  • Works for any real numbers, including decimals and negatives
  • Rounding is applied to 4 decimal places
  • Percent change formulas divide by the original value, so the original cannot be zero

Frequently asked questions

How do I calculate percent change?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value, then multiply by 100. If the result is positive it's an increase; if negative, a decrease.

What is the difference between percentage points and percent?

A change from 10% to 12% is a 2 percentage point increase but a 20% relative increase (2 ÷ 10). Use percentage points when comparing rates directly.

How do I find the original number from a percentage?

If X% of a number equals Y, the original number is Y ÷ (X ÷ 100). For example, if 15% of a number is 30, the number is 30 ÷ 0.15 = 200.
