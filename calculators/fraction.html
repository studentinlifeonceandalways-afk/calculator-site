Fraction Calculator

Use this fraction calculator to add, subtract, multiply, or divide two fractions. Enter the numerator and denominator of each fraction, choose an operation, and get the result in simplest form. For mixed numbers, convert to improper fractions first (e.g., 2 1/2 → 5/2).

Worked example

1/2 + 1/3:

  • Result: 5/6

Find a common denominator (6), then add 3/6 + 2/6 = 5/6.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Denominators cannot be zero
  • Result is simplified to lowest terms via GCD
  • For mixed numbers, convert to improper fractions before entering
  • Results are displayed as exact fractions, not decimals

How the calculation works

a/b + c/d = (ad + bc) / bd

a/b − c/d = (ad − bc) / bd

a/b × c/d = ac / bd

a/b ÷ c/d = ad / bc

Then divide numerator and denominator by their greatest common divisor.

Frequently asked questions

Can I enter mixed numbers?

Convert to improper fractions first. For example, 2 1/2 = 5/2 (2 × 2 + 1 = 5 over 2).

How is the result simplified?

The numerator and denominator are divided by their greatest common divisor (GCD) using Euclid's algorithm.

What if the result is improper?

It's shown as an improper fraction (e.g., 7/4). To convert: 7/4 = 1 3/4. Divide numerator by denominator for the whole number, remainder over denominator for the fraction.
