Rent vs Buy Calculator

Use this rent vs buy calculator to compare the monthly cost of renting with the monthly cost of owning a home. It estimates ownership cost from mortgage principal and interest, property tax, insurance, and a maintenance allowance, then compares it to rent. This is a simplified comparison — it does not include closing costs, tax deductions, or home appreciation.

Worked example

$2,000 rent vs $400k home, 20% down, 6.5%, 30 years, 1.2% tax:

  • Monthly P&I: $2,022.62
  • Monthly tax: $400.00
  • Monthly maint + insurance: $433.33
  • Total ownership: $2,855.95

Assumptions and limitations

  • Maintenance and insurance estimated at 1% and 0.3% of home price per year
  • Does not include closing costs, HOA, or PMI
  • Ignores tax deductions and home appreciation
  • Compares monthly cash cost only, not total wealth

How the calculation works

Loan = Price × (1 − Down%)

P&I = standard amortization formula

Total = P&I + (Price × tax%)/12 + (Price × 1.3%)/12

Frequently asked questions

Is this a complete rent vs buy analysis?

No. It's a monthly cash-flow comparison. A full analysis should include opportunity cost of the down payment, tax deductions, appreciation, and transaction costs.

Why does owning look more expensive here?

Because it includes costs renters don't pay — tax, maintenance, insurance. It also builds equity, which this comparison doesn't capture.

What if I put less than 20% down?

You'll typically pay PMI (0.3–1.5% of loan per year), which this calculator does not include.
