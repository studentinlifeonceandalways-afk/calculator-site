Square Footage Calculator

Use this square footage calculator to find the area of a rectangular space from its length and width in feet. It returns square feet and square yards, useful for flooring, paint, sod, and rental listings. For irregular rooms, break the space into rectangles, calculate each one, and add the results.

Worked example

12 ft × 10 ft room:

  • Square feet: 120 sq ft
  • Square yards: 13.33 sq yd

For flooring, add 5–10% for waste and cuts.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Rectangular shape only; for L-shapes or irregular rooms, split into rectangles
  • Does not subtract built-ins, columns, or alcoves
  • Does not include waste factor — add 5–10% for materials

How the calculation works

Square feet = Length (ft) × Width (ft)

Square yards = Square feet ÷ 9

Frequently asked questions

How do I measure an irregular room?

Divide the room into rectangles, calculate the square footage of each, and sum them. For rooms with alcoves or bays, treat each section separately.

How many square feet is a 12x12 room?

144 square feet. 12 × 12 = 144. That's 16 square yards.

How much extra should I buy for flooring?

Add 5–10% for standard layouts, 10–15% for diagonal or herringbone patterns, and more for rooms with many corners or offsets.
