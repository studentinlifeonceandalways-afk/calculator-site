Compound Interest Calculator

Use this compound interest calculator to estimate the future value of a lump-sum investment from the starting principal, annual interest rate, number of years, and how often interest compounds. Compound interest means you earn interest on your interest, so more frequent compounding and longer time horizons produce disproportionately larger growth. This calculator assumes no additional contributions and a fixed rate.

Worked example

$10,000 at 7% for 10 years, compounded monthly:

  • Future value: $20,096.61
  • Total interest earned: $10,096.61
  • Effective annual rate: 7.23%

Assumptions and limitations

  • Fixed annual rate for the entire period
  • No additional contributions or withdrawals
  • No taxes, fees, or inflation adjustment
  • Compounding frequency applies consistently for all years

How the calculation works

FV = P × (1 + r/n)n·t

Where P is principal, r is annual rate as a decimal, n is compounding periods per year, and t is years.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between simple and compound interest?

Simple interest is calculated only on the original principal. Compound interest is calculated on principal plus accumulated interest, so growth accelerates over time.

Does more frequent compounding always mean more money?

Yes, but with diminishing returns. Moving from annual to monthly compounding adds more than moving from monthly to daily.

How do I include regular monthly contributions?

This calculator handles lump sums only. For monthly contributions, use a future value of annuity calculator or sum results from separate lump sums.
