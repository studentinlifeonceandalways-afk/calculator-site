Age Calculator

Use this age calculator to find exact age in years, months, and days from a date of birth to today. It also shows total months and total days. Useful for forms, anniversaries, and eligibility checks where exact age matters.

Worked example

Born May 10, 1990; today November 23, 2026:

  • Exact age: 36 years, 6 months, 13 days
  • Total months: 438
  • Total days: 13,346

Assumptions and limitations

  • Uses the local time zone of your device
  • Leap years handled automatically
  • Months are calendar months, not fixed 30-day periods

How the calculation works

Subtracts the birth date from today's date in calendar units: years, then months, then days, borrowing from the previous month when the day difference is negative.

Frequently asked questions

Does this account for leap years?

Yes. Leap years are handled automatically because the calculation uses real calendar dates.

Why does the "months" count differ from a simple division?

Calendar months vary in length. This calculator counts full calendar months.

Can I calculate age on a future date?

Not with this version — it calculates age as of today.
