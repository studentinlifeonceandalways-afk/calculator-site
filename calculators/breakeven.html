Break-Even Calculator

Use this break-even calculator to find the number of units you must sell, and the revenue required, to cover total costs. Break-even is where total revenue equals total costs — profit is zero at that point. Enter fixed costs, variable cost per unit, and selling price per unit. Above break-even, each sale contributes to profit at the contribution margin.

Worked example

$10,000 fixed, $20 variable, $50 price:

  • Contribution margin: $30
  • Break-even units: 334
  • Break-even revenue: $16,700

Assumptions and limitations

  • Fixed costs stay constant across the relevant range of output
  • Variable cost and selling price are constant
  • Selling price must exceed variable cost, or break-even is impossible

How the calculation works

Contribution margin = Price − Variable cost

Break-even units = Fixed costs ÷ Contribution margin

Break-even revenue = Units × Price

Frequently asked questions

What counts as fixed vs variable cost?

Fixed costs don't change with volume — rent, salaries. Variable costs rise with each unit — materials, packaging.

What if my selling price is below variable cost?

Break-even is impossible — every sale loses money. The calculator will show an error.

How do I include target profit?

Add target profit to fixed costs: units = (Fixed + Target profit) ÷ Contribution margin.
