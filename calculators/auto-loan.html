Auto Loan Calculator

Use this auto loan calculator to estimate your monthly car payment, the total interest you'll pay, and the total cost of the loan. Enter the amount financed, the annual interest rate, and the loan term in months. The calculator assumes a fixed-rate installment loan with equal monthly payments and does not include taxes, title fees, dealer fees, or trade-in value — add those to the financed amount for a realistic estimate.

Worked example

$25,000 at 6.5% for 60 months:

  • Monthly payment: $489.13
  • Total paid: $29,347.60
  • Total interest: $4,347.60

Extending the term to 72 months lowers the monthly payment but raises total interest.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Fixed rate for the entire loan term
  • Equal monthly payments; no extra payments or early payoff
  • Does not include sales tax, title fees, dealer fees, or gap insurance
  • Does not include trade-in value or down payment — subtract those from the amount financed

How the calculation works

M = P × r(1+r)n / ((1+r)n − 1)

Where P is the amount financed, r is the monthly rate (annual ÷ 12), and n is the number of monthly payments. Same amortization formula as a mortgage.

Frequently asked questions

Should I choose a 36, 48, 60, or 72 month term?

Shorter terms have higher monthly payments but less total interest. 60 months is the most common; 72–84 months lower the payment but can leave you owing more than the car is worth early in the loan (negative equity).

Does this include sales tax and fees?

No. Add sales tax, title, registration, and dealer fees to the amount financed to get a realistic monthly payment. Many buyers roll those costs into the loan.

What about a down payment or trade-in?

Subtract the down payment and trade-in value from the vehicle price before entering the amount financed. The result is the principal that gets amortized.

How does my credit score affect the rate?

Auto loan rates vary widely by credit tier. This calculator uses the rate you enter; check current lender rate sheets for typical APR ranges by credit score.
