BMI Calculator

Use this BMI calculator to estimate your Body Mass Index from your height and weight in metric units. BMI is a screening tool that compares your weight to a range considered healthy for your height in adults 20 and older. It is not a diagnostic tool and does not measure body fat directly, muscle mass, or distribution of fat.

Worked example

70 kg at 175 cm:

  • BMI: 22.9
  • Category: Normal weight

BMI categories for adults

  • Underweight: below 18.5
  • Normal weight: 18.5 – 24.9
  • Overweight: 25.0 – 29.9
  • Obese: 30.0 and above

Assumptions and limitations

  • Intended for adults 20 and older; not valid for children or teens
  • Does not distinguish muscle from fat
  • Does not account for age, sex, ethnicity, or fat distribution
  • Not a substitute for medical advice

How the calculation works

BMI = weight (kg) ÷ height (m)2

Height in centimeters is divided by 100 to convert to meters before squaring.

Frequently asked questions

Is BMI accurate for everyone?

No. BMI can misclassify muscular athletes as overweight and may not reflect health risk accurately across different ethnic groups.

Should I use BMI for children?

No. Children and teens require age- and sex-specific BMI percentile charts.

What is a healthy BMI?

For most adults, 18.5–24.9 is considered normal. Individual health depends on many factors beyond BMI.
