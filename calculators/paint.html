Paint Calculator

Use this paint calculator to estimate how many gallons of paint you need for a room. Enter length, width, wall height, number of doors and windows, and number of coats. It assumes standard coverage of 350 square feet per gallon, typical for interior latex paint on a primed surface.

Worked example

12×10 room, 8 ft walls, 1 door, 2 windows, 2 coats:

  • Gross wall area: 352 sq ft
  • Paintable area: 301 sq ft
  • Total for 2 coats: 602 sq ft
  • Gallons: 2

Assumptions and limitations

  • Standard coverage: 350 sq ft per gallon
  • Door ≈ 21 sq ft; window ≈ 15 sq ft
  • Does not include ceiling or trim
  • Round up to whole gallons

How the calculation works

Wall area = 2 × (L + W) × H

Paintable = Wall area − (Doors × 21) − (Windows × 15)

Gallons = (Paintable × Coats) ÷ 350

Frequently asked questions

How much area does a gallon of paint cover?

Most interior latex paints cover 350–400 sq ft per gallon per coat on smooth, primed surfaces.

Should I include the ceiling?

This calculator covers walls only. Add Length × Width for ceilings separately.

Why buy extra paint?

Buying 10% extra is common to allow for touch-ups and avoid running out mid-job.
