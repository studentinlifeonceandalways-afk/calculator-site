Recipe Scaler

Use this recipe scaler to adjust an ingredient quantity when you change the number of servings a recipe makes. Enter the original quantity, the original serving count, and the new serving count. The calculator returns the scaled quantity and the scale factor.

Worked example

2 cups for 4 servings, scaled to 6 servings:

  • Scale factor: 1.5×
  • New quantity: 3 cups

Assumptions and limitations

  • Assumes the recipe scales linearly
  • Does not adjust for evaporation, pan size, or leavening
  • Does not handle unit conversion

How the calculation works

Scale factor = New servings ÷ Original servings

New quantity = Original quantity × Scale factor

Frequently asked questions

Do all ingredients scale the same way?

No. Salt, spices, leavening agents, and alcohol often need less than a linear scale-up.

Can I scale a recipe down?

Yes. Enter a new serving count smaller than the original and the scale factor will be below 1.

Does this convert cups to grams?

No. This scales the number only. Use the Unit Converter for unit conversion.
