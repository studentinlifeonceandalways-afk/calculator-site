Mortgage Payment Calculator

Use this mortgage payment calculator to estimate your monthly principal and interest payment, the total interest paid over the life of the loan, and the total cost of the mortgage. Enter your loan amount, annual interest rate, and loan term in years. The calculator assumes a fixed-rate mortgage with monthly payments and does not include property taxes, homeowners insurance, HOA fees, or PMI — add those separately for a full monthly housing cost picture.

Worked example

$300,000 loan at 6.5% for 30 years:

  • Monthly payment: $1,896.20
  • Total paid over 30 years: $682,632
  • Total interest: $382,632

Roughly 56% of what you pay over the life of this loan is interest.

Assumptions and limitations

  • Fixed interest rate for the entire loan term
  • Monthly compounding; payments made at the end of each month
  • Does not include property tax, homeowners insurance, PMI, HOA, or closing costs
  • Does not account for extra payments, biweekly payments, or refinancing
  • Results are estimates for planning, not a lender quote

How the calculation works

Formula: M = P × r(1+r)n / ((1+r)n − 1)

Where P is the loan principal, r is the monthly interest rate (annual rate ÷ 12), and n is the total number of monthly payments (years × 12).

Frequently asked questions

Does this include property taxes and insurance?

No. This calculator shows principal and interest only. To estimate your full monthly payment, add your annual property tax and homeowners insurance, divide by 12, and add that to the result.

What is the difference between interest rate and APR?

The interest rate is what you pay on the outstanding loan balance. APR includes the interest rate plus lender fees and points, so it is usually higher and better for comparing loan offers.

How does a 15-year term compare to 30 years?

A 15-year loan has a higher monthly payment but roughly half the total interest of a 30-year loan at the same rate. Change the loan term above to compare both scenarios.
