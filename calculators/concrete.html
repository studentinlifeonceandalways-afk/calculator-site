Concrete Volume Calculator

Use this concrete calculator to estimate the volume of concrete needed for a rectangular slab in cubic feet and cubic yards, plus the number of 60 lb or 80 lb pre-mixed bags. Enter the length, width, and thickness of the slab. Add 5–10% for waste and spillage when ordering.

Worked example

10 ft × 10 ft slab at 4 inches thick:

  • Volume: 33.33 cu ft (1.23 cu yd)
  • 60 lb bags: 75
  • 80 lb bags: 56

Assumptions and limitations

  • Assumes rectangular slab of uniform thickness
  • Bag yields: 60 lb ≈ 0.45 cu ft; 80 lb ≈ 0.60 cu ft
  • Does not include waste — add 5–10%

How the calculation works

Volume (cu ft) = Length × Width × Thickness (ft)

Volume (cu yd) = Volume (cu ft) ÷ 27

Bags = Volume ÷ bag yield

Frequently asked questions

How much extra concrete should I order?

Add 5–10% for typical slab work. Running short mid-pour is far more costly than a small overage.

How many 80 lb bags are in a cubic yard?

About 45 bags of 80 lb concrete per cubic yard.

Does this work for footings or columns?

No. This calculator is for rectangular slabs only.
