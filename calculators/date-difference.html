Date Difference Calculator

Use this date difference calculator to find the number of days, weeks, months, and years between two dates. It also shows the calendar difference in years, months, and days. Useful for project timelines, contract terms, vacation planning, and deadline calculations.

Worked example

January 1, 2026 → December 31, 2026:

  • Days: 364
  • Weeks: 52.0
  • Calendar difference: 0 years, 11 months, 30 days

Assumptions and limitations

  • Uses the device's local time zone
  • Excludes the end date from the day count (difference between midnight of each date)
  • Calendar months and years account for leap years and month lengths
  • Order does not matter — the calculator swaps dates to return a positive result

How the calculation works

Days = floor((end − start) ÷ 86,400,000 ms)

Weeks = Days ÷ 7

Calendar difference = years, months, and days calculated from calendar fields (handles leap years and month lengths)

Frequently asked questions

Does the calculator include the end date?

No. It returns the difference between the two dates (end minus start), not inclusive day count. Add 1 if you need the inclusive count.

How does it handle leap years?

Leap years are handled automatically because the calculation uses real calendar dates, not a fixed 365-day year.

Can I calculate business days?

Not with this version — it counts calendar days. For business days excluding weekends and holidays, use a dedicated working-days calculator.
